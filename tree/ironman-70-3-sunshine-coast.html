Ironman 70.3 Sunshine Coast in 2026: a 1.9 km swim, a 90 km ride and a 21.1 km run, back to back. I raced it on a $1,300 second-hand road bike, and my first time riding with cleats was three weeks before.

I was super pumped from the start. Down on the beach I was psyching myself up for the swim, bouncing with energy from all the carb loading. The swim itself was easy and felt like a warm-up.

Transition to the bike was wet. It was raining, there was mud all over the floor, and everything was an inch under water by the time I got to it. Transition to the run was the same.

The bike was good: wet and windy. I was pretty slow next to everyone on aerodynamic bikes, and I definitely need a better bike next time. Still, for the amount of prep I'd done, I was pretty happy.

The first 4 km of the run were brutal. My ankle-stabilising muscles were on fire, and after a kilometre they stopped responding. I eventually shook it off around 4 km and worked my way to the finish.

The whole way, my friends were on the sidelines cheering me on. It made the whole event so much fun and gave me such an energy boost. They're the best ❤️

I did it with my mate Victor, and we both came out with almost the same time. It was an awesome event to train for and finish with a mate 💪
